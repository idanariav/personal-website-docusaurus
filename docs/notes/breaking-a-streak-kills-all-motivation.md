# F-it effect

## Notes

### Claim
When a [streak](/notes/momentum-is-movement-that-builds-self-reinforcing-forward-action.md) is lost or a [rule](/notes/absolute-rules-trade-flexibility-for-willpower-savings.md) is broken, there's no meaning to it anymore, so we lose all motivation and all restraints.

### Explanation

### Why it matters

### Examples
For example, let's say that you had a "no meat" diet that you kept for 100 days. Either by mistake or a moment of weakness, you consume a tiny bite of meat. Once you realize this, you feel that there is no going back, that your diet is over, so there's no difference between eating a bit of meat or a whole lot, so you go on a carnivore rampage [Emotional hijacking is uncontrolled System 1 dominance over reasoning](/notes/emotional-hijacking-is-uncontrolled-system-1-dominance-over-reasoning.md).

### Opposers
The antidote is [Strong urges are short-lived if you wait them out](/notes/strong-urges-are-short-lived-if-you-wait-them-out.md), letting the urge to abandon everything pass instead of acting on it in the moment.
