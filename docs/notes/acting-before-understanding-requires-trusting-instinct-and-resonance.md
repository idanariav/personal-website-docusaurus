# Leap Into Faith

## Notes

### Claim
Leap into faith means that some decisions in life can't be the result of a [rational process](/notes/truth-is-discoverable-through-reason-and-logic-alone.md), that some [truths](/notes/truth.md) are beyond our [reasoning](/notes/system-2-enables-deliberate-analytical-thinking-for-complex-problems.md) skills, that we need to act or believe before we understand. This is often the result of feeling that something "is just right", even if we can't explain it. Sometimes we just have to [Self-trust is the first step to trusting others](/notes/self-trust-is-the-first-step-to-trusting-others.md) our [Instincts](/notes/heuristics-are-mental-shortcuts-enabling-fast-automatic-decisions.md), our body, our feelings and be [courageous](/notes/standing-up-for-yourself-is-courageous-authenticity-against-social-pressure.md), to follow through despite [Uncertainty creates anxiety when knowledge and control are incomplete](/notes/uncertainty-creates-anxiety-when-knowledge-and-control-are-incomplete.md) and doubt, even when we don't know fully [why](/notes/self-awareness-reveals-mental-and-emotional-states-enabling-intentional-action.md), yet it [resonates](/notes/resonance-is-being-in-sync-with-reality-and-others.md) deeply within us.

### Explanation
We should remember that not all knowledge is intellectual, even our thoughts [go beyond](/notes/thinking-extends-beyond-the-brain-to-environment-and-society.md) the limits of our physical brain, and some types of knowledge are expressed through [emotions](/notes/emotions-provide-decision-relevant-information-when-navigating-choices.md) and [expriences](/notes/some-knowledge-requires-direct-experience-to-fully-understand.md), all are relevant to our lives and who we are.

### Why it matters
Living can't be on reason alone, it would often lead to [Too much information prevents decision-making](/notes/too-much-information-prevents-decision-making.md). Same as [morality](/notes/morality-is-both-rational-and-emotional.md), it requires also heart and soul.

[Intuition is trained expertise converted to automatic response](/notes/intuition-is-trained-expertise-converted-to-automatic-response.md) complicates this though, what feels like a leap beyond reason might really just be reason compressed into something automatic, trained expertise rather than something separate from or above it.

### Open questions
How do you tell apart a genuine leap of faith from [Impulsivity is acting without conscious deliberation or filter](/notes/impulsivity-is-acting-without-conscious-deliberation-or-filter.md)? Both mean overriding the rational side and letting instinct take the wheel, and it's not always clear in the moment which one you're doing.
